# BrightDesk IT Solutions — Knowledge Base

## Company Overview
BrightDesk IT Solutions is a managed IT services company founded in 2018 and headquartered in Manchester, UK. The company supports around 120 small and medium-sized businesses across the North West of England. BrightDesk has 45 staff and offices in Manchester and Leeds. Its mission is to give small businesses enterprise-grade IT support at an affordable monthly price.

## Service: DeskCare
DeskCare is BrightDesk's core support plan. It includes unlimited remote help desk support, device monitoring and monthly security patching. Support is available Monday to Friday, 8am to 6pm. Price: £35 per user per month.

## Service: CloudGuard
CloudGuard is a backup and disaster recovery service. Client data is backed up every 4 hours to an encrypted UK data centre and kept for 90 days. In an outage, CloudGuard aims to restore critical systems within 8 hours. Price: £15 per user per month.

## Service: SecureStart
SecureStart is a cyber security package for small businesses. It includes quarterly vulnerability scans, phishing simulation emails and help achieving Cyber Essentials certification. Price: £20 per user per month.

## Policy: Passwords
All BrightDesk staff and client accounts must use passwords of at least 14 characters. Multi-factor authentication (MFA) is required for email, VPN and all admin accounts. Passwords must never be shared over email or chat.

## Policy: Laptops and Devices
New staff receive a company laptop on their first day. Laptops are replaced every 4 years. Personal devices may only access company email through the approved mobile app. A lost or stolen laptop must be reported to the service desk within 1 hour.

## Policy: Remote Access (VPN)
Staff working from home must connect through the company VPN to access internal systems. The VPN requires MFA at every login. VPN issues should be raised as a priority ticket.

## Support Hours and Response Times
The service desk is open Monday to Friday, 8am to 6pm. Out-of-hours emergency support is available only to clients on the CloudGuard plan. Response targets: Priority 1 (system down) within 30 minutes, Priority 2 within 2 hours, Priority 3 within 1 business day.

## Frequently Asked Questions
- How do I raise a ticket? Email support@brightdesk.example or call the service desk during support hours.
- Can I get support at the weekend? Only emergency support is available at weekends, and only for CloudGuard clients.
- Who do I contact about my contract? Your account manager.
